Gameloft Toronto + Montreal Sick Kids Charity Stream

I assisted the Gameloft Toronto and Montreal studios in creating stream graphics for their 2023 Sick Kids charity stream.
The stream was a resounding success! Thanks to all the team members who participated in the stream and the generous viewers, we were able to raise $13,000 for the foundation!
I created the following visuals for our streamers to use, inspired by our studios industrial architecture, brand colours, and Sick Kids neon branding.

I also made the following posts for our social media in both English and french.
Huge props to Renne Wong for helping organize the event and to Katerina Oltinik for creating the traffic driving posts!

Gameloft Toronto Social Club Mascot

After having taken part in many of their workplace events and movie screenings, it was an honor to design Gameloft Toronto’s Social club mascot!

Meet Terry the trash panda!
“This lil' guy kinda showed up in the office out of nowhere. We're not sure how they got in... but no one's taken issue since they're just so cute and friendly! They love board games, snacks, and creating opportunities for everyone to spend time together. Whether it's spreading the word about an upcoming movie night, or brainstorming the next rock climbing outing, Terry's here to help with their scrappy resourcefulness and can-do attitude. Terry's terrifically excited to be part of GL Toronto's social club team!”
